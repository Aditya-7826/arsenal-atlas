## Notes

- Belt-fed machine guns (mg-34, mg-42, m60, fn-mag, pkm) have `capacity: null` on purpose, because a belt has no fixed capacity. They are not uncertain.
- `nickname` and `designer` are null where there is no widely used nickname or no single credited designer.
- Figures with several common variants: m16 uses M16A1 figures; m4-carbine length is with the stock extended; galil capacity uses the standard 35-round magazine; hk-ump calibre and capacity use the .45 ACP version; aa-12 year is for the original 1972 design; l115a3 year is the British Army adoption year.
- Worth a second check even though filled in: walther-p38 (adoption year 1938), tac-50 (year is the Canadian adoption year, 2000), insas (standard capacity 20).
